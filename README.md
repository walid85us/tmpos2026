## Why TM POS2026

Small shops often juggle a till, a repair log, a stock spreadsheet and a customer notebook, and none of them talk to each other. TM POS2026 brings that work into one workspace, built for businesses that sell, repair and service in the same place.

- **One workspace for the whole shop.** Sales, repairs, inventory, customers and staff in one place, instead of separate tools.
- **Built for repair and retail together.** Repair tickets, warranties, returns and parts sit alongside ordinary checkout.
- **Grows with the business.** Plans and add-ons switch modules on as the shop needs them.
- **Role-based access.** Each team member sees and does only what their role allows.
- **Security by design.** Access checks on the server and an audit trail for important changes are built into the platform's foundation.

## For service providers

TM POS2026 is designed as a multi-business platform. The organisation that offers it to shops gets its own management tools:

- **Business accounts:** onboard the shops it serves, and rename, suspend or reactivate them.
- **Plans and billing:** plans, feature matrix, add-ons, subscriptions and usage.
- **Provisioning and domains:** set up new businesses.
- **Security and oversight:** a platform team with its own roles, multi-factor sign-in for administrators, and an audit record of administrative actions.
- **Command Center:** a summary of the platform at a glance.

---

## Security and trust

TM POS2026 is being built with these protections at its core:

- **Separation between businesses.** Each business's data and activity are kept apart from every other business's.
- **Server-side access control.** Roles and permissions are enforced by the platform itself, not just hidden in the screen.
- **Protected administration.** Administrators use a separate console with multi-factor authentication.
- **Accountability.** Important administrative changes are recorded in an audit trail.
- **Abuse protection.** Request limits protect accounts and services from automated attacks.
- **Continuous quality checks.** Every change is checked by automated tests and security scans.
